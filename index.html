Common questions

Quick answers about offline usage, storage, and custom question banks.

Do I need an account?

No. Every subject opens immediately with zero sign-ups, logins, or tracking servers.

Where is my progress saved?

Your answers, accuracy stats, missed questions, and bookmarks are stored locally in your browser per subject. You can also export or import a JSON backup anytime from the quiz sidebar.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once visited online, the Service Worker caches the workspace and question banks for offline use—and you can also run the app directly from local files via file://.

Can I use my own questions?

Yes. Open any subject workspace and click Upload to drag-and-drop your own .json or .js question bank, complete with starter templates and instant validation.

Offline & Local Storage

Browser Persistence

All quiz answers, accuracy stats, missed lists, bookmarks, and theme settings are saved locally in localStorage per subject—nothing is ever sent to a server.

Offline Caching & Portable Backups

When opened over HTTPS, the Service Worker caches the workspace and visited banks for offline use. You can also export or import a portable .json backup anytime from the quiz sidebar.
